§ 01 - THE GRANT

What this tier grants

  1. Who may use it. A natural person who is a student (undergraduate, master's, or PhD), faculty or staff of an academic institution, a non-profit or independent researcher, or an instructor using the software with a course cohort.
  2. Purpose limit. Non-commercial academic use only: coursework, dissertations, theses, teaching, and research that is not paid client work and not commercial delivery. Work that funds revenue is outside this tier.
  3. What is granted. The free-tier rights in section 3 of the licence, plus use of the pro-tier product jgs-magic-sysmlv1-pro, for that purpose. No licence key is required for this tier unless the acceptance reply says otherwise.
  4. What is not granted. The restrictions in section 4 are not waived. This tier does not grant a right to redistribute the software, and it does not grant a right to ship the software to anyone else.
  5. Term. Twelve months from the date of the acceptance reply: the email that grants access. Renewal is a fresh submission of the academic request form and a fresh attestation. It is not an automatic extension.
  6. Citation. A citation or acknowledgement is requested. It is not a condition of the grant.
  7. Revocation. The grant stays revocable under sections 2 and 9 of the licence. Breach of section 4, or use outside the purpose limit, ends it.
  8. Version. This section's version is 2026-10. A later edit that changes who qualifies, the purpose limit, the term, or the inherited restrictions takes a new YYYY-MM version. Typos and contact-detail fixes do not.
